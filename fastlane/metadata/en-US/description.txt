Niu Lai: Escape in the Fog is a fully offline survival-horror game.

The sun never sets, and the things in the fog never stop. Choose Niu Lai, Big Horn, or Baola, then search for a way out of a grassland that grows stranger with every step.

Features:
• Five connected escape chapters
• Grasslands, a forest of fog, the Snow Night of No Return, the Otherworld, and the final Beast Tide
• Three characters with different difficulty, route length, and enemy numbers
• Stamina management, crawling sprint, three hearts, and visible injury states
• Random rain and snow, sudden flashes, unsettling music, and proximity audio
• Several enemy types that also hunt animals along the road
• Safe zones that slowly lose their warmth
• Local high scores recording distance and time to the millisecond
• No account, no ads, and no internet connection required

Cross the fog. Do not look back. They have already seen you.

Warning: contains flashing images, screen shake, horror audio, and stylized fantasy violence. Players sensitive to flashing lights should use caution.
